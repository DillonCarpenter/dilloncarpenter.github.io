    My Project

    Chess motif detecting Neural Network
    April 24, 2026 · map[name:Dillon Carpenter]

    Goals:#

    Use Lichess’ public chess puzzles database to construct a CNN that can detect various motifs.

    What is the problem to solve?#

    Modern chess engines excel at calculating optimal moves but often fail to explain why those moves are strong in terms that humans understand. This project aims to bridge that gap by building a neural network capable of detecting tactical and positional motifs in chess positions.

    Why this problem is important to solve?#

    Because chess engines are designed to simply give raw evaluations, this makes it difficult for learners and even advanced players to understand the underlying ideas behind recommend moves.

    How do I solve this problem?#

    Using a large dataset of annotated chess puzzles, the system will analyze board states and output the motifs present in each position as a multi-label classification problem. The end goal is to create a fast, interpretable system that enhances chess learning by identifying patterns such as forks, pins, and discovered attacks.
